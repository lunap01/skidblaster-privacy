Frequently Asked Questions

Tilt controls aren't responding.

Make sure you've allowed motion access when prompted at the start of a game. If you accidentally denied it, go to your device's Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness and make sure Skid Blaster is turned on. You can also just drag on the screen to aim if you'd rather not use tilt at all — both control methods work throughout the whole game.

I can't hear any sound effects or music.

First, check your phone's physical mute/silent switch — Skid Blaster is set up to play sound even in silent mode, but it's worth ruling out. Next, check the speaker icon in the top-left corner of the game screen to make sure it's not toggled to mute. If sound still isn't working, try force-closing and reopening the app.

How do I clean multiple skid marks with one shot?

Line your stream up so it grazes past more than one mark in a straight line — if the marks are roughly in line with each other from your aiming position, a single held shot can clean both at once. This becomes especially useful on later levels where ammo is limited.

What is "dribble mode"?

On the final levels, your stream pressure runs out entirely, and you're left with only short-range dribbles. You'll need to adjust your distance to get up close, and tap repeatedly rather than holding down, to clean what's left. It's meant to be the toughest part of the game — hang in there.

The game seems stuck or frozen.

Try closing the app completely (swipe it away from your app switcher) and reopening it. If a freeze happens repeatedly at a specific point, please let us know using the contact details below — details like which level you were on and what you'd just done are really helpful for tracking down the cause.

Can I change my character's appearance?

Yes — on the start screen, before you tap "Step Up," you can choose a skin tone and a scale for your character. These are purely cosmetic and don't affect gameplay difficulty.

Does Skid Blaster require an internet connection?

No. The entire game runs on your device and works fully offline.

Does the app collect any of my data?

No — Skid Blaster doesn't collect, store, or transmit any personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
