Privacy Policy for Skid Blaster

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Skid Blaster ("the App") is developed by Paul Scicluna ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App. We've kept this short because the App genuinely doesn't do much with data — please read on for the specifics.

The short version

Skid Blaster does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no network connection of any kind. Everything the App does happens entirely on your device.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect, and the App does not transmit to us or to any third party, any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information — the App has no accounts or sign-in
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other device content
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers — the App shows no ads
  • Payment or purchase information — the App has no in-app purchases

Device motion data

The App requests access to your device's motion sensors (Core Motion) to power its tilt-based aiming controls. This data:

  • Is used only in real time, on your device, to control gameplay
  • Is never stored, logged, saved to disk, or transmitted anywhere
  • Is never linked to you personally

You can decline or revoke motion access at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness. The App remains playable using on-screen touch controls if motion access is not granted.

No internet connection required

Skid Blaster does not connect to the internet during normal use. It contains no third-party software development kits (SDKs) for analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

Children's privacy

Skid Blaster is rated 18+ on the App Store due to its crude humor and content, and is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, and this is especially true for children under the age of 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it — though as noted above, the App does not collect information from any user in the first place.

Data retention

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to retain, store, or delete. All game state (your progress, settings, and preferences) lives only in the App's memory on your device for the duration of your play session and is not saved between sessions or shared anywhere.

Changes to this policy

If this Privacy Policy changes — for example, if a future version of the App adds a feature that does involve data collection — we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
